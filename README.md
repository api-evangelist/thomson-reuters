# Thomson Reuters

Thomson Reuters provides over 137 APIs across legal, tax and accounting, risk and fraud, and trade and supply industries through their global developer portal. APIs cover Westlaw legal research, Checkpoint tax content, ONESOURCE tax software, HighQ document management, Legal Tracker matter management, and risk and fraud solutions.

**Developer Portal:** [https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/](https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/)

## APIs

| Name | Description | Documentation |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| [Westlaw API](https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/) | Legal research content including case law, statutes, litigation analytics, SEC filings, and court dockets. | [Docs](https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/) |
| [Checkpoint API](https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/) | Tax and accounting research content for tax professionals and accountants. | [Docs](https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/) |
| [ONESOURCE API](https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/) | Tax compliance software integration for income tax, indirect tax, and global trade. | [Docs](https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/) |
| [HighQ API](https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/) | Legal document management and client collaboration platform integration. | [Docs](https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/) |
| [Legal Tracker API](https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/) | Legal matter management, e-billing, and legal spend management. | [Docs](https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/) |
| [Risk and Fraud API](https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/) | Identity verification, KYC, AML, and adverse media screening. | [Docs](https://developers.thomsonreuters.com/) |
