A comprehensive overview defining direct tax as levies paid directly to government that cannot be shifted to others, covering individual income tax, corporate income tax, capital gains, estate tax, property tax, and payroll taxes. It explains tax provision calculations, state apportionment complexities, and distinguishes direct taxes from indirect taxes, emphasizing how technology improves compliance accuracy across multiple jurisdictions.
