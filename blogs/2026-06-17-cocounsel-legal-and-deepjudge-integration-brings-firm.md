Thomson Reuters announces the integration of CoCounsel Legal with DeepJudge, enabling attorneys to access their firm's institutional knowledge alongside trusted legal authority in a single workflow. Users can search firm materials via DeepJudge and attach documents directly to CoCounsel sessions, combining firm precedent with Westlaw and Practical Law resources for enhanced drafting, analysis, and research.
