Based on interviews with over 2,400 general counsels, this analysis addresses the visibility gap between legal departments' self-perception and C-suite recognition. Key findings include technology discussions doubling from 14% to 28% (with 86% mentioning AI), persistent resource constraints, and the need for legal teams to communicate business outcomes rather than internal tasks to gain organizational visibility and resources.
