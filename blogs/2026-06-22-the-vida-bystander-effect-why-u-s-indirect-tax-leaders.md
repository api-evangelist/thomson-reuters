Survey data reveals that while 86% of EU professionals are confident in ViDA compliance, only 35% understand actual requirements, and 78% of organizations lack centralized ViDA governance with 54% concerned about invoice rejection and non-payment by customers. The article addresses the "ViDA bystander effect"—where widespread confidence masks inadequate preparation—and urges U.S. indirect tax leaders to establish centralized programs for EU VAT digital reporting compliance.
