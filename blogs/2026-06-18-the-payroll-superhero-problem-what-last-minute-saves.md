This article examines how frequent last-minute payroll rescues signal deeper operational failures rather than competent management, with reactive workflows increasing compliance errors and audit exposure. Industry expert Ian Giles explains that payroll teams absorb upstream failures, and the piece emphasizes that standardized processes and automation reduce dependency on key individuals and create resilient operations.
